Privacy

Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information Pixel Tools may process when you use the site and the browser-based tools it provides.

Overview

How data is handled

  • Pixel Tools is intended to work primarily in the browser.
  • Uploaded images and tilemaps are processed locally in your browser unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.
  • Project data may be stored in browser local storage so you can reopen work on the same device.
  • If you contact the site owner by email or social platforms, your message and contact details may be retained to reply to your request.

Advertising

Third-party services

If advertising or analytics services are enabled on Pixel Tools, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. Users should review the relevant third-party policies for more details about their practices.

Requests

Your choices

You can clear locally stored project data through your browser storage settings or by using the delete and reset actions available in the map editor. For privacy-related questions, contact dkhoankieu@gmail.com.
